Host a World

Built-in multiplayer sharing

  1. Launch the selected version and create or open a singleplayer world.
  2. Open the pause menu and choose the world-sharing option, such as Share to LAN or Open to LAN.
  3. Copy the invite code and send it to your friends. They should select the same version and join from Multiplayer.

Keep the game open while hosting. Friends can join over the internet through the game's relay; this is a temporary world, not a 24/7 dedicated server.

World sharing is supported here for Vanilla 1.5.2, Vanilla 1.8.8, and Eaglercraft 26.3 WASM. Choose one of those versions to host.
